## What the tempo actually tells us

Twenty minutes at 3:51/km, average HR 157 against an easy-run HR of ~139
and a 5K race HR up around 172–175. That is a controlled, sub-threshold
effort held *faster than half-marathon goal pace* — and you describe it
as really comfortable. That is a genuinely strong indicator, and it
supports the lower end of the 1:20–1:24 prediction band.

Two honest caveats before we rewrite anything:

1. **The Puma Fast R 3s are worth something.** Max-stack carbon plate,
   typically 2–4% economy. At this pace that's roughly 5–8 sec/km, so the
   trainer-equivalent is around 3:56–3:59/km. Still excellent — right at
   threshold — but it isn't evidence you're ten seconds a kilometre ahead
   of the model.
2. **It's one session, three weeks after a 5K.** Goal pace gets revised
   on a fortnight of evidence, not one Saturday.

Practical note: keep most threshold work in normal session shoes. It
keeps your pace references honest, saves the Fast R 3s' limited life for
race day, and avoids doing every hard session in one loading pattern.
Use them for one dress rehearsal in early September and at Cheltenham.
