## The session that needs a conversation

**Saturday 1 August: 11.9 km at 3:51/km average, HR 157.**

The plan asked for 20 minutes of continuous tempo — comfortably hard,
explicitly "a reintroduction, not a test" — inside an easy 8–10 km.
What the watch recorded is 11.9 km averaged at **3:51/km**. For context
your 10K PB is 38:06, which is 3:49/km. That is essentially 10K race
pace held for nearly 12 km, three weeks after a 5K race.

There are two readings and I can't tell them apart from the data alone:

1. **You are fitter than the model thinks.** Averaging 3:51/km at
   HR 157 — well below threshold — while targeting 3:54/km for the half
   would be a genuinely excellent sign, and would mean 1:22 is
   conservative.
2. **You turned a reintroduction into a race.** In which case the cost
   shows up later, not on the day.

Sunday's numbers lean slightly toward (2): after Saturday, plus a 14 km
long run in **28.2 °C** (high heat risk, with your asthma history), this
morning's readings are the softest of the week — HRV **34** (week's
lowest, against a 43.9 average), RHR **50** (highest), sleep score 68.
Nothing alarming, exactly what a hard weekend in heat should produce,
and Monday is already a rest day. But it's the first genuine dip of the
build.

acute dip lands on a rest day. Week 3 as written (rest Monday, easy
